NAME

scan.boston - the city's radio, written down

SYNOPSIS

scan [--hear] [--ask question] [--brief hours] [--map]

DESCRIPTION

Listens to Boston's police, fire, EMS and transit radio around the clock. Transcribes every transmission, works out where it happened, threads it into incidents and keeps the audio, so a newsroom can find out what the scanner said while nobody was listening.

Built for the night desk, the 3am page, and whoever walks in at 6 wondering what they missed.

OPTIONS

--hear
Every transcript line carries its audio. Misheard a street? Play the four seconds it came from and decide for yourself.
--ask "what happened on storrow last night"
Query the archive in plain English. Returns whole scenes with timelines, sources and audio, rather than a pile of loose lines.
--brief 10
Shift Change: what is still open, what was major, everything above routine. A handoff for whoever is next.
--map
Live calls on a map, each one carrying how sure the system is about where it happened. Refuses to draw a pin it cannot actually place.

CAVEATS

Machine-transcribed, machine-located, unverified. A lead, never a fact. Reporters confirm with the department before anything runs.

Not for publication. Not a public service. Newsroom only.

BUGS

Mishears street names, unit numbers and anything said over a siren. Cannot hear encrypted channels, so some incidents go quiet partway through. Both are reasons the audio is kept.

ACCESS

Accounts are handed out one at a time. To get one:

$ slack @matt-karolian "add me to scan.boston"

In plain terms: send Matt Karolian a Slack message.
